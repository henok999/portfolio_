About Me

I am Henok Debebe Abera, a professional IT expert, a passionate HVAC Engineer, Digital Marketer, Graphics Designer and Web Developer from Ethiopia.
I specialize in building professional websites, voting systems, digital marketing campaigns and creative graphic designs.

I am a creative writer, book designer, and web developer with experience in both publishing and digital projects. I have designed the interior layouts of more than six books and created cover designs for several published works. My writing experience includes over 30 short stories and social media scripts, and I have also contributed to a collaborative poetry anthology with fellow writers.
Among my notable projects, I designed the interior layout of Nefs Bota, a poetry collection created by ten renowned poets, and the first edition of Re'es Yelew by the acclaimed poet Elias Shitahun. In addition to my publishing work, I developed a pilot online election website for the Sidama Regional Election Board and enhanced the functionality and user experience of a school website.
I am passionate about combining creativity, design, and technology to produce engaging print and digital experiences.
My goal is to combine engineering knowledge with modern technology to create innovative and effective digital solutions.
